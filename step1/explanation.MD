**Bruteforce Attacks**

Bruteforce attacks are the most basic entryways for breaches.
A simple script of sending (somewhat) random combinations of the most used passwords.
There are many textfiles online which offer a neatly compiled array of the most used passwords ever.
The most often used one is the rockyou file.

If you have a confirmed email adress, that you know is a user in the database you want to bruteforce,
you try every single password in that file, together with the known email adress (or username).
If the website doesn't have a ratelimit, or prevention measures to combat this, you can try millions of passwords in a relatively short timespan.
